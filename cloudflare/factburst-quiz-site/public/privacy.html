Privacy notice

This notice explains what information Factburst Quiz uses, why it is used, and how advertising consent is managed.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Privacy at a glance

You can play quizzes without creating an account. If you create an account, information is used to provide features such as saved scores, history, rankings, comments, friends and challenges. Advertising consent for eligible users is handled through Google's Privacy & messaging service.

1. Information Factburst Quiz may process

Account information

If you register, the service may process information such as your username, email address, account credentials, verification status and account settings so that your account can be created, authenticated and maintained.

Quiz and profile activity

The service may process quiz attempts, best scores, accuracy, rankings, quiz history, achievements, saved quizzes, reactions and other progress information associated with your account.

Community activity

If you use social features, the service may process comments, friend relationships, friend requests, challenges, live-match activity, reports and related moderation information.

Technical information

Like most online services, technical request information may be processed when you access the site, such as device or browser information, request timing, network information and security-related logs needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How information is used

  • to provide quizzes and calculate results;
  • to create and secure user accounts;
  • to save scores, history and profile progress when account features are enabled;
  • to provide leaderboards, comments, friends, challenges and related community features;
  • to detect abuse, diagnose faults and protect the service;
  • to improve the reliability and usefulness of Factburst Quiz; and
  • to display advertising where permitted by the user's consent choices and applicable law.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Factburst Quiz uses essential site storage for core functionality and may use optional advertising technology on supported quiz pages.

Essential storage

Essential storage may be used for core account functionality, security and normal operation of the service.

Advertising and consent

On supported quiz pages, Factburst Quiz can use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google’s Privacy & messaging European regulations message is used to collect and manage consent for eligible users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. The message is operated by Google's certified consent management solution and supports the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers, web beacons, IP addresses and related information in accordance with the user's consent choices, Google's policies and applicable law. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Users can review or change their advertising consent choices through the Privacy & messaging controls presented by Google. Google also provides a consent revocation mechanism for approved sites using its European regulations messages.

4. Advertising choices

You can control Google's personalised advertising settings through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about some third-party advertising opt-out choices through YourAdChoices / aboutads.info.

Turning off personalised advertising does not necessarily remove all advertising. Google may still serve non-personalised or contextual advertising, subject to the user's choices and applicable law.

5. Third-party services

Factburst Quiz may rely on infrastructure and third-party services to operate the website and may link to external platforms such as YouTube. When advertising is enabled, Google AdSense is also a third-party service involved in delivering advertisements.

Third-party sites and services have their own terms and privacy practices. Opening a third-party link takes you outside Factburst Quiz.

6. How long information is kept

Information is kept for as long as reasonably needed to provide the relevant feature, maintain account and service integrity, resolve disputes, meet applicable legal obligations, or protect the service from abuse. Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

7. Your choices and rights

You can choose not to create an account and can use available guest quiz features without saving account-based history. You can also manage advertising consent through the Google Privacy & messaging controls when they are presented.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of personal information. Account controls available within your Factburst profile should be used where they provide the relevant action.

8. Children

Factburst Quiz is a general-audience quiz service. If local law requires parental or guardian consent for a young person to use account or data-processing features, that consent should be obtained before those features are used.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect the service and account information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should keep their account access details private and use strong, unique passwords.

10. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when Factburst Quiz changes its features, providers or privacy practices. The date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

11. Terms

Use of the service is also subject to the Factburst Quiz Terms of Use.
