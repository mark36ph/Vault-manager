Factburst Quiz FAQ

Find out how the quizzes work, how scores are calculated and what to do if you find a problem.

How many questions are in a quiz?

Most Factburst challenges are designed as short ten-question quizzes. The exact question count is shown on each quiz before and during play.

How is my score calculated?

Your score is the number of questions you answer correctly. The results screen also shows the total and percentage so you can compare performances between quizzes.

What happens after I finish?

You can review your result and, where an explanation is available, read the factual reason behind an answer. You can then play another quiz or compare scores on the leaderboard.

Which subjects are available?

Factburst includes categories such as science, history, geography, space, nature and animals, technology, arts and literature, music, film, sports, entertainment, mathematics and general knowledge.

Can I report a question?

Yes. If a question appears incorrect, unclear or otherwise problematic, use the reporting option provided with the quiz. Reports are reviewed as part of ongoing content quality work.

Do I need an account to play?

Core quiz play is designed to be straightforward. Account features are used for experiences such as profiles, saved scores and other site features when available.
