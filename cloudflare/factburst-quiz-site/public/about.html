About Factburst

Fast quizzes built around real facts.

Factburst Quiz is a quiz site for people who enjoy learning something useful while testing what they already know.

What Factburst is for

Each Factburst challenge is designed as a short burst of questions rather than a long lesson. The aim is simple: give you a clear topic, ask fair multiple-choice questions, and provide factual explanations after you finish.

The library covers subjects such as science, history, geography, space, technology, nature, music, film, sport, mathematics and general knowledge. That mix makes the site useful both for focused practice and for discovering subjects you may not normally explore.

Our approach

Questions first, explanations second

1. Test your knowledge

Every quiz presents a sequence of multiple-choice questions so you can commit to an answer before seeing the result.

2. Learn from the result

After completing a quiz, the result screen can show factual explanations for the questions, turning mistakes into opportunities to learn.

3. Keep exploring

Category pages and related quizzes make it easy to continue with another challenge on the same subject.

Content quality matters

Factburst is built around original quiz material rather than scraped articles or copied question banks. Questions are stored with their answer choices and explanations so the quiz can be checked, played and improved over time.

If you spot a question that looks incorrect or unclear, use the available reporting tools on the quiz to flag it. Feedback helps keep the library useful.
