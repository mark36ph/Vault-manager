How it works

A fair way to compare your best results

Your best result matters

The leaderboard uses each player's strongest saved result for a quiz. If you replay a challenge and improve your score, the better result can replace the earlier one.

Every quiz is a fresh test

Factburst quizzes cover different subjects and question sets. Playing across several categories gives you a broader way to test your general knowledge.

Use scores as a challenge

A leaderboard position is only one measure of performance. The useful part is seeing which subjects you know well and which quizzes give you something new to learn.

Keep learning

Improve your score by exploring more subjects

Try a mixture of science, history, geography, space, technology, nature, music, film, sports, mathematics and general knowledge. When a quiz provides explanations, use them to understand an answer rather than relying only on the final score.

After a strong result, choose another category and see whether you can transfer what you know to a completely different topic. Factburst is designed to make short quiz sessions useful as well as competitive.
